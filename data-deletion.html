Data Deletion Instructions

BlackRing Investment Agent — last updated 6 September 2026

Your right to delete

You may request deletion of your BlackRing account and the personal data we hold about you at any time. We honour deletion requests promptly, typically within 30 days.

Option A — Request deletion in the App (recommended)

  1. Log in to the BlackRing dashboard.
  2. Open Settings (your profile).
  3. Use the account deletion option (or contact support with your registered account identifier / email).

Option B — Request deletion by email

Send an email from the address linked to your account to danchan999@hotmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request and include:

  • Your full name,
  • The email or Facebook/Google identifier used to register,
  • Confirmation that you wish your account and data to be deleted.

We will verify ownership and complete the deletion, then confirm to you by email.

Option C — Delete through Facebook directly

If you registered with Facebook and wish to remove the App’s connection from Facebook’s side:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Security and login → Apps and websites).
  2. Find BlackRing and select Remove.

Removing the app in Facebook revokes the connection. To also delete data held by BlackRing, please also use Option A or B above.

What gets deleted

Your account record, profile details, and data associated with the account. Retention backups are purged on their normal cycle. Some data may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes (e.g. fraud prevention), after which it is deleted.
