Privacy Policy

BlackRing Investment Agent (“BlackRing”, “we”, “us”) — last updated 6 September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information BlackRing collects when you use the BlackRing investment-agent dashboard application (the “App”), how we use it, and the choices you have. By registering or logging in through the App you agree to the practices in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Registration data. When you register or sign in with Facebook or Google, we receive your public profile identifier from that provider (e.g. your Facebook/Google user ID), your name, and — where the provider shares it — your email address and profile picture. We do not receive or store your Facebook/Google password.
  • Account data. Information you provide in the App (e.g. name, preferred markets, notification contacts such as Telegram chat ID or WhatsApp phone number that you choose to add).
  • Authenticator data. If you activate two-step login, we store only a verification reference for your Authenticator app — never your codes or secrets in plaintext.
  • Usage data. Basic logs (login timestamps, feature activity) used to keep the App secure and operational.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To provide the App’s features: market analysis, watchlists, schedules, reports and notifications you request.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Third-Party Providers

When you use “Register with Facebook” or “Continue with Google”, authentication is handled by Meta Platforms, Inc. and Google LLC respectively under their own privacy policies. We receive only the profile data described above.

5. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored on our own infrastructure. Secrets and API keys are never stored or displayed in plaintext. We apply access controls and standard security measures; no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices & Rights

  • You may update your profile information in the App’s Settings at any time.
  • You may request deletion of your account and data by contacting us at the address below.
  • Where applicable law grants you rights (access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability), you may exercise them by contacting us.

7. Data Deletion

You can request removal of your account and associated data at any time. See the Data Deletion page for instructions, including how to delete your account directly through Facebook.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “last updated” date above and, where practical, notifying registered users.
